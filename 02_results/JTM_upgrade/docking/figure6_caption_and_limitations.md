## 2. Recommended Methods Correction (replacing line 77)

> Structure-based docking was performed with AutoDock Vina 1.2.7 [44] across 11 human gutMGene v2.0 target candidates (AlphaFold DB monomer v2.0 models [45] for *IDO1* [P14902], *PEMT* [Q9UBM1], *FFAR2* [O15552], and *TGFBR1* [P36897], and RCSB PDB Chain A crystal structures `5NI9` [1.33 A], `8WRA` [1.45 A], `3QF2` [1.70 A], `3BHY` [1.24 A], `1JDH` [1.90 A], `3SOE` [1.60 A], and `6BSC` [1.30 A]) and 11 representative microbial metabolites (121 pairs). Neutral PubChem isomeric SMILES were embedded in 3D via RDKit (v2026.09.2; ETKDGv3, `randomSeed = 2026`, MMFF94/UFF minimization) and converted to PDBQT with Meeko (v0.8.0; `MoleculePreparation`); receptors were prepared via `mk_prepare_receptor.py` (`--default_altloc A --allow_bad_res`) after removing solvent/heteroatoms (`HETATM = 0`) and residues with AlphaFold `pLDDT < 60`. Focused 26 × 26 × 26 A boxes centered on chain-A non-solvent co-crystal ligands were used for *DAPK3* (`7CP`) and `5NI9` (`MRD`), whereas automated folded-core boxes (up to 60 × 60 × 60 A) were applied to the remaining nine apo receptors (`exhaustiveness = 16`, `seed = 2026`, `n_poses = 5`). Heavy-atom contacts (<= 4.0 A) and polar contacts (<= 3.55 A) were extracted from top-ranked poses. Structural quality control comprised per-residue AlphaFold pLDDT and UniProt topology mapping, crystal superimposition against holo-IDO1 (`2D0T`, `4PK5`) and apo-IDO1 (`9FOH`), 5-seed repeatability and box-size sensitivity checks (`PEMT` + urolithin A; `IDO1` + indole-3-carboxylic acid), and symmetry-corrected heavy-atom RMSD redocking of `A1IEE` (`iDeg-2`) into `9FOH` Chain A. Docking scores were treated strictly as hypothesis-generating structural plausibility evidence without cofactor parameterization, membrane embedding, or molecular dynamics refinement.

## 3. Recommended Discussion Limitation Item (to add under Limitations, lines 173–179)

> **7. Receptor Cofactor Omission, Search-Box Inflation, Membrane Absence, and PDB Chain-Selection Constraints in Molecular Docking:**
> Independent structural quality control identified important physical and methodological constraints in the rigid-receptor Vina screen (`02_results/JTM_upgrade/docking/`). First, although pocket contact residues in the AlphaFold *PEMT* (`Q9UBM1`, contact pLDDT 87.94–96.25, mean 93.03) and *IDO1* (`P14902`, contact pLDDT 88.88–98.69, mean 97.22) models exhibit high local backbone confidence, both receptors were docked in vacuo without essential cofactors or membrane context. *IDO1* defaulted from mmCIF-only `9FOH` (1.60 A) to the heme-free AlphaFold model, and all `HETATM` records were stripped (`Meeko 0.8.0` lacks `HEM` residue templates and `autogrid4`/`AD4` grid generation was unavailable); consequently, top-ranked indole-3-carboxylic acid poses (-6.68 kcal/mol) occupy the vacated heme pocket (0.95–1.01 A heavy-atom clash with superimposed `2D0T`/`4PK5` heme; 3.43 A from proximal ligand `HIS346`), overlapping the apo-IDO1 inhibitor `A1IEE` (`9FOH`, 0.32 A) and precluding concurrent heme binding without invoking unproven cofactor displacement. Similarly, *PEMT* is a 4-helix ER/MAM integral membrane enzyme (`84` transmembrane/intramembrane residues, mean pLDDT 97.29) docked without a phospholipid bilayer or S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM; `4.10 A` from catalytic `GLU180`), yielding a near-isoenergetic alternative pose inside the transmembrane bundle (-7.76 vs. -7.84 kcal/mol; 12.31 A shift). Second, 9 of 11 targets used blind folded-core search boxes exceeding `27,000 A^3` (`35,297–216,000 A^3`). While focused `26 A` redocking of `A1IEE` into apo-IDO1 `9FOH` Chain A achieved `0.63–0.65 A` symmetry-corrected RMSD across `5/5` ETKDGv3 seeds (-13.04 +- 0.02 kcal/mol), blind `FoldedCore_Box` redocking (`156,247 A^3`) failed (`9.68 A` RMSD), and `IDO1` + indole-3-carboxylic acid flipped by `4.34–4.36 A` RMSD between two isoenergetic orientations (`<= 0.04 kcal/mol` gap) across seeds and focused grids. Third, neutral SMILES protonation omitted carboxylate ionization at pH 7.4, truncating `pLDDT < 60` loop `363–376, 380` in `IDO1` introduced backbone breaks, and retaining only PDB Chain A mis-mapped two heterocomplex crystal entries (`5NI9` docked `HLA-DRA` rather than the 15-aa `ENO1` peptide on Chain C; `1JDH` docked beta-catenin rather than `TCF7L2` on Chain B) and split the `MUC1` SEA heterodimer (`6BSC`). Accordingly, docking findings are retained solely as exploratory structural hypotheses requiring holo-enzyme, membrane-embedded, and experimental biophysical validation.
